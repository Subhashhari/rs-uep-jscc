# Insights

- Based on 5 channel seed(s); 2441 images per seed. All gains below are paired per seed against the baseline (same packet losses), so they carry no luck-of-the-draw advantage.
- Break-even: the best protected method overtakes `uniform` from about erasure_rate = 0.39. ("Best" is picked per erasure_rate on THIS split, so it is a descriptive envelope, not a validation-selected method; quote validation-selected schemes in papers.)
- Low-loss penalty: even the best method is 8.22 dB below `uniform` at erasure_rate = 0.3 (two_tier_50_50@predicted). Two causes: tiers with little or no parity (the unprotected bottom tier of the 25/50/25 scheme loses its packets outright) and shorter RS blocks than the single uniform block (finite block length); experiments/coding_controls.py separates them.
- Peak gain: +3.59 dB at erasure_rate = 0.45 by three_tier_25_50_25@l1 (95% CI ±0.12, significant).
- Importance sources ranked by their best scheme's mean gain over erasure_rate >= 0.35: predicted +0.30 dB, l1 +0.29 dB, oracle +0.29 dB, energy +0.27 dB, random -0.44 dB.
- Share of the reference ranking's gain captured: predicted 105%, l1 102%, energy 95%, random -153%. A share above 100% means that source allocated protection better than the reference.
- Random-order control gains -0.44 dB versus oracle +0.29 dB, so the benefit comes from *which* channels are protected, not merely from tiering.
- Worst-case images at erasure_rate = 0.4: 5th-percentile PSNR 16.30 dB (three_tier_25_50_25@predicted) vs 14.29 dB (uniform).
- Worst-case images at erasure_rate = 0.5: 5th-percentile PSNR 12.11 dB (three_tier_25_25_50@energy) vs 11.86 dB (uniform).
- Seed sensitivity: largest std across channel seeds is 0.41 dB (uniform, erasure_rate = 0.35); gains smaller than that are not reliable.
- `uniform` shows its steepest fall (-19.57 dB) between erasure_rate = 0.3 and 0.35 -- its cliff.
