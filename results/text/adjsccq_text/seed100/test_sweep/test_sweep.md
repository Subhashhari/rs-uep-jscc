# Insights

- Based on 5 channel seed(s); 2441 images per seed. All gains below are paired per seed against the baseline (same packet losses), so they carry no luck-of-the-draw advantage.
- Break-even: the best protected method overtakes `uniform` from about erasure_rate = 0.37. ("Best" is picked per erasure_rate on THIS split, so it is a descriptive envelope, not a validation-selected method; quote validation-selected schemes in papers.)
- Low-loss penalty: even the best method is 3.62 dB below `uniform` at erasure_rate = 0.3 (two_tier_50_50@energy). Two causes: tiers with little or no parity (the unprotected bottom tier of the 25/50/25 scheme loses its packets outright) and shorter RS blocks than the single uniform block (finite block length); experiments/coding_controls.py separates them.
- Peak gain: +2.48 dB at erasure_rate = 0.45 by three_tier_25_50_25@energy (95% CI ±0.09, significant).
- Importance sources ranked by their best scheme's mean gain over erasure_rate >= 0.35: oracle +1.09 dB, energy +1.08 dB, attention +0.71 dB, random +0.22 dB.
- Share of the reference ranking's gain captured: energy 99%, attention 65%, random 21%. A share above 100% means that source allocated protection better than the reference.
- Random-order control gains +0.22 dB versus oracle +1.09 dB, so the benefit comes from *which* channels are protected, not merely from tiering.
- Worst-case images at erasure_rate = 0.4: 5th-percentile PSNR 17.42 dB (three_tier_25_50_25@oracle) vs 15.73 dB (uniform).
- Worst-case images at erasure_rate = 0.5: 5th-percentile PSNR 14.66 dB (three_tier_25_25_50@energy) vs 14.34 dB (uniform).
- Seed sensitivity: std across channel seeds stays below 0.3 dB everywhere.
- `uniform` shows its steepest fall (-9.39 dB) between erasure_rate = 0.3 and 0.35 -- its cliff.
