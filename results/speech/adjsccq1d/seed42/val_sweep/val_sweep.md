# Insights

- Based on 3 channel seed(s); 4000 images per seed. All gains below are paired per seed against the baseline (same packet losses), so they carry no luck-of-the-draw advantage.
- Break-even: the best protected method overtakes `uniform` from about erasure_rate = 0.32.
- Low-loss penalty: even the best method is 0.58 dB below `uniform` at erasure_rate = 0.3 (three_tier_25_50_25@energy). This is the block-length effect of a single large RS block, which tiered coding gives up.
- Peak gain: +2.24 dB at erasure_rate = 0.45 by three_tier_25_25_50@l1 (95% CI ±0.09, significant).
- Importance sources ranked by their best scheme's mean gain over erasure_rate >= 0.35: l1 +1.78 dB, energy +1.76 dB, predicted +1.75 dB, oracle +1.65 dB, attention +1.19 dB, random +0.58 dB.
- Share of the reference ranking's gain captured: l1 107%, energy 106%, predicted 106%, attention 72%, random 35%. A share above 100% means that source allocated protection better than the reference.
- Random-order control gains +0.58 dB versus oracle +1.65 dB, so the benefit comes from *which* channels are protected, not merely from tiering.
- Worst-case images at erasure_rate = 0.4: 5th-percentile PSNR -0.52 dB (three_tier_25_50_25@l1) vs -3.96 dB (uniform).
- Worst-case images at erasure_rate = 0.5: 5th-percentile PSNR -4.71 dB (three_tier_25_25_50@predicted) vs -5.43 dB (uniform).
- Seed sensitivity: std across channel seeds stays below 0.3 dB everywhere.
- `uniform` shows its steepest fall (-2.72 dB) between erasure_rate = 0.3 and 0.35 -- its cliff.
