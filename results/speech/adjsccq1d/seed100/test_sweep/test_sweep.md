# Insights

- Based on 5 channel seed(s); 4000 images per seed. All gains below are paired per seed against the baseline (same packet losses), so they carry no luck-of-the-draw advantage.
- Break-even: the best protected method overtakes `uniform` from about erasure_rate = 0.32.
- Low-loss penalty: even the best method is 0.65 dB below `uniform` at erasure_rate = 0.3 (two_tier_50_50@attention). This is the block-length effect of a single large RS block, which tiered coding gives up.
- Peak gain: +1.83 dB at erasure_rate = 0.45 by three_tier_25_25_50@oracle (95% CI ±0.04, significant).
- Importance sources ranked by their best scheme's mean gain over erasure_rate >= 0.35: oracle +1.39 dB, energy +1.29 dB, random +0.51 dB, attention +0.18 dB.
- Share of the reference ranking's gain captured: energy 93%, random 36%, attention 13%. A share above 100% means that source allocated protection better than the reference.
- Random-order control gains +0.51 dB versus oracle +1.39 dB, so the benefit comes from *which* channels are protected, not merely from tiering.
- Worst-case images at erasure_rate = 0.4: 5th-percentile PSNR -0.81 dB (three_tier_25_50_25@oracle) vs -3.48 dB (uniform).
- Worst-case images at erasure_rate = 0.5: 5th-percentile PSNR -4.54 dB (three_tier_25_25_50@oracle) vs -5.06 dB (uniform).
- Seed sensitivity: std across channel seeds stays below 0.3 dB everywhere.
- `uniform` shows its steepest fall (-2.47 dB) between erasure_rate = 0.3 and 0.35 -- its cliff.
