# Insights

- Based on 3 channel seed(s); 4000 images per seed. All gains below are paired per seed against the baseline (same packet losses), so they carry no luck-of-the-draw advantage.
- Break-even: the best protected method overtakes `uniform` from about erasure_rate = 0.31.
- Low-loss penalty: even the best method is 0.69 dB below `uniform` at erasure_rate = 0.3 (three_tier_25_50_25@energy). This is the block-length effect of a single large RS block, which tiered coding gives up.
- Peak gain: +3.33 dB at erasure_rate = 0.4 by three_tier_25_25_50@oracle (95% CI ±0.22, significant).
- Importance sources ranked by their best scheme's mean gain over erasure_rate >= 0.35: oracle +2.50 dB, predicted +2.47 dB, energy +2.36 dB, l1 +2.32 dB, random +0.06 dB.
- Share of the reference ranking's gain captured: predicted 99%, energy 94%, l1 93%, random 2%. A share above 100% means that source allocated protection better than the reference.
- Random-order control gains +0.06 dB versus oracle +2.50 dB, so the benefit comes from *which* channels are protected, not merely from tiering.
- Worst-case images at erasure_rate = 0.4: 5th-percentile PSNR -7.57 dB (three_tier_25_25_50@oracle) vs -12.45 dB (uniform).
- Worst-case images at erasure_rate = 0.5: 5th-percentile PSNR -11.17 dB (three_tier_25_25_50@oracle) vs -13.43 dB (uniform).
- Seed sensitivity: std across channel seeds stays below 0.3 dB everywhere.
- `uniform` shows its steepest fall (-4.51 dB) between erasure_rate = 0.3 and 0.35 -- its cliff.
