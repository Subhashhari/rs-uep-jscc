# Insights

- Based on 5 channel seed(s); 4000 images per seed. All gains below are paired per seed against the baseline (same packet losses), so they carry no luck-of-the-draw advantage.
- Break-even: the best protected method overtakes `uniform` from about erasure_rate = 0.32.
- Low-loss penalty: even the best method is 0.71 dB below `uniform` at erasure_rate = 0.3 (three_tier_25_50_25@energy). This is the block-length effect of a single large RS block, which tiered coding gives up.
- Peak gain: +2.94 dB at erasure_rate = 0.4 by three_tier_25_50_25@energy (95% CI ±0.06, significant).
- Importance sources ranked by their best scheme's mean gain over erasure_rate >= 0.35: energy +2.30 dB, oracle +2.27 dB, random +0.28 dB.
- Share of the reference ranking's gain captured: energy 101%, random 12%. A share above 100% means that source allocated protection better than the reference.
- Random-order control gains +0.28 dB versus oracle +2.27 dB, so the benefit comes from *which* channels are protected, not merely from tiering.
- Worst-case images at erasure_rate = 0.4: 5th-percentile PSNR -4.98 dB (three_tier_25_50_25@energy) vs -9.08 dB (uniform).
- Worst-case images at erasure_rate = 0.5: 5th-percentile PSNR -8.79 dB (three_tier_25_25_50@energy) vs -10.18 dB (uniform).
- Seed sensitivity: std across channel seeds stays below 0.3 dB everywhere.
- `uniform` shows its steepest fall (-3.81 dB) between erasure_rate = 0.3 and 0.35 -- its cliff.
